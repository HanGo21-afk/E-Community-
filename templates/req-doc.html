Document Information

Barangay Clearance

Fee ₱20.00
Processing Time 1-3 Days

Requirements

  • Valid Government ID (Original and photocopy)
  • Cedula (Community Tax Certificate) - Current year
  • 1x1 or 2x2 Photo - Recent, white background
  • Proof of Residency (if new resident in the barangay)

Certificate of Residency

Fee ₱20.00
Processing Time 1-3 Days

Requirements

  • Valid Government ID (with current address or original and photocopy)
  • Proof of Residence (Utility bill, lease contract, or land title)
  • Cedula (Optional but may be required)

Certificate of Indigency

Fee Free
Processing Time Same Day

Requirements

  • Valid Government ID (Original and photocopy)
  • Proof of Residency (Must be resident of the barangay)
  • Supporting Documents (Medical abstract, school enrollment form, etc.)

Business Permit Endorsement

Fee ₱100-200
Processing Time 1-3 Days

Requirements

  • DTI Business Name Registration (for sole proprietorship)
  • SEC Registration (for corporations/partnerships)
  • Valid Government ID (Owner or authorized representative)
  • Lease Contract or Land Title (Proof of business location)
  • Sketch or Location Map (Some barangays require)
  • Cedula (Community Tax Certificate)

Cedula (Community Tax Certificate)

Fee ₱5.00
Processing Time Same Day

Who Can Avail

  • Individuals who have reached 18 years of age
  • Individuals earning income (employed or self-employed)
  • Corporation or juridical entities
  • Real property owners
  • Anyone engaging in business

First-Time Jobseeker Certification

Fee Free
Processing Time Same Day

Who Can Avail

  • Filipino citizen actively seeking employment
  • Have not been employed before (no payslip history)
  • Resident of the barangay for at least 6 months
  • Graduate of any educational level (K-12, college, TESDA)

Step-by-Step Process

1

Visit the Barangay Hall

Visit during office hours. Prepare and bring all required documents for your specific document type.

2

Submit Your Documents

Present all requirements to the barangay staff. If documents were already uploaded through this form, inform the staff. They will verify the submitted documents for completeness.

3

Pay the Required Fee

Complete the payment according to the fee schedule. Some documents are free (Certificate of Indigency, Cedula, First-Time Jobseeker Certification).

4

Wait for Processing

Your document will be processed according to the processing time listed. Same-day documents are usually available within hours. Others may take 1-3 business days.

5

Collect Your Document

Once processing is complete, collect your document from the barangay hall. You may be notified via email or phone if registration was provided.
